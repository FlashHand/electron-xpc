# Review xpc-007-2 — caller context, re-verify

Second independent review (fresh agent, 2026-10-18) of the fixes from [xpc-007-1](xpc-007-1.md). Electron
`v43.4.1` source: `electron_api_ipc_handler_impl.cc:178-182` sets the event's `frameId` to the sending frame's
`GetRoutingID()` and `processId` to its process id; `electron_api_web_frame_main.cc:448-457` reads the same two
values, so they compare directly and a renderer cannot forge them.
